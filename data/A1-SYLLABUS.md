# A1 syllabus and release scope

This original course maps to CEFR A1 communicative outcomes. CEFR describes abilities; it does not prescribe a single German chapter sequence or grammar list. This module sequence is our teaching design.

24 units: 18 taught lessons and 6 checkpoints. Each has six steps: outcomes; dialogue, reading and listening; vocabulary; patterns; guided practice; writing and speaking. Allow repeated practice and real conversation beyond the estimated lesson time. Course completion is not a certified A1 proficiency result.

## A1.1 — First interactions

Introduce yourself and exchange basic personal details with a patient speaker.

Descriptor scales: Conversation; Information exchange; Notes, messages and forms.

- 1.1 Greetings & introductions: Choose a greeting and say goodbye.
- 1.2 Origin, residence & languages: Say where you come from and where you live.
- 1.3 Numbers, spelling & personal details: Understand and give numbers from 0 to 100 in familiar contexts.
- 1.4 Module 1 checkpoint: meet a course partner: Understand key details in short text and slow speech.

## A1.2 — People, home & work

Give simple information about people, a home and familiar work or study.

Descriptor scales: Sustained monologue: describing experience; Reading for orientation; Information exchange.

- 2.1 Family & people: Introduce close family members.
- 2.2 Home & possessions: Describe a home and name its main rooms.
- 2.3 Work, study & everyday places: Say what you do and where you work or study.
- 2.4 Module 2 checkpoint: a new neighbour: Understand key details in short text and slow speech.

## A1.3 — Daily life & plans

Describe familiar activities and arrange a simple meeting using times and days.

Descriptor scales: Information exchange; Overall spoken interaction; Correspondence.

- 3.1 Daily routine: Describe a simple sequence of daily activities.
- 3.2 Time, dates & appointments: Understand simple times, weekdays and dates.
- 3.3 Leisure, weather & simple plans: Say what you like doing and what you can do.
- 3.4 Module 3 checkpoint: arrange a weekend: Understand key details in short text and slow speech.

## A1.4 — Food, shopping & transactions

Request familiar goods, understand quantities and prices, and complete a simple purchase.

Descriptor scales: Obtaining goods and services; Reading for orientation; Understanding an interlocutor.

- 4.1 Food, drink & a café order: Order a simple drink or snack politely.
- 4.2 Groceries, quantities & prices: Buy everyday food using simple quantities.
- 4.3 Clothes, preferences & requests: Ask for a simple item in a shop.
- 4.4 Module 4 checkpoint: shopping day: Understand key details in short text and slow speech.

## A1.5 — Travel, places & services

Find a place, use basic transport information and ask for a simple service.

Descriptor scales: Understanding an interlocutor; Reading for orientation; Obtaining goods and services.

- 5.1 Around town & simple directions: Ask where a familiar place is.
- 5.2 Transport & travel information: Buy a simple train or bus ticket.
- 5.3 Accommodation & simple service problems: Check into a hotel using simple details.
- 5.4 Module 5 checkpoint: arrive in a new city: Understand key details in short text and slow speech.

## A1.6 — Needs, messages & consolidation

Express a basic need, exchange short messages and combine familiar language across A1 situations.

Descriptor scales: Correspondence; Notes, messages and forms; Information exchange; Mediating a text.

- 6.1 Health, needs & asking for help: Say how you feel and name a simple symptom.
- 6.2 Short messages, invitations & forms: Understand a brief everyday message.
- 6.3 A short account & A1 consolidation: Give a very short account of a familiar experience using learned phrases.
- 6.4 A1 final checkpoint: everyday communication: Understand key details in short text and slow speech.

## Assessment and progression

Every taught unit includes reading, synthetic-speech listening, guided language use, two written answers, an original writing task and an oral task. Checkpoints combine prior learning; the final checkpoint samples all six modules. All guided tasks must be attempted and at least 80% correct. Checkpoint feedback follows submission and retry creates a fresh attempt. Writing and speaking use learner self-checks and models, not automatic proficiency claims. Transcripts and model use are recorded as supported practice. A device German voice is required for audio; text support remains available. All 24 units must be complete before A1 course completion is shown. A2–C2 remain future content. Previous Unit 1.1 completion and points are preserved, with an invitation to review expanded material.
